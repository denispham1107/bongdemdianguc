[ban 1] binh mau / binh mana + sach phep chu to, cuon

A. ti le roi binh
A1. 3000 con quai chet: 282 binh mau (9.4%), 290 binh mana (9.7%) - mong 10% moi loai
A2. giet that 24 con: roi 6 binh, nam dung cho quai chet (<= 1 m) 6/6

B. nhat binh (choi mot minh)
B1. binh cach 6 m (ban kinh hut 3.5 m) sau 1 giay: van nam yen = True
B2. dua binh vao 2,5 m: bay vao nguoi sau 0.33 giay, so binh mau = 1

C. uong binh
C1. CHUA MO KHOA, co 1 binh: hoi 0, con 1 binh
C2. mo khoa: cap binh mau 1/1, con 1 diem, nang cap tiep duoc = False
C3. thieu 250 mau: hoi 200 (mau 543.5 -> 743.5), con 2 binh
C4. uong tiep NGAY: hoi 0, con 2 binh (hoi chieu 0,5 giay)
C5. sau 0,6 giay, thieu 30 mau: hoi 30, mau 793.5/793.5, con 1 binh
C6. mau day: hoi 0, con 1 binh (khong phi binh)
C7. CastAt(binh mana), thieu 120 nang luong: 182.5 -> 257.5 (+75), con 1 binh
C8. thieu 20 nang luong: hoi 20, con 0 binh
C9. HET binh mana: hoi 0

D. so binh hien tren o ky nang
D1. sach phep (may tinh): 3 binh -> 12 binh, o binh doi 0.0499, o doi chung doi 0.0000
D1. sach phep (cam ung): 3 binh -> 12 binh, o binh doi 0.0412, o doi chung doi 0.0000

E. sach phep: chu to va cuon
E1. ten ky nang cot trai: net that 65 diem; doi chung co cu 54, co moi (x1.15) 65 -> that / cu = 1.204
E2. dong cap phan than chi tiet: net that 184 diem; doi chung co cu 167, co moi (x1.2) 185 -> that / cu = 1.102
E3. than chi tiet: noi dung cao 128 / vung 111 diem; dat cuon 100000 -> kep con 22 (toi da 22); anh doi: than 0.0980, dau muc 0.0001
E4. man 844x390: cot trai cao 268, 9 hang can 536 -> phai cuon True (vua 9 hang)
E4. man 640x300: cot trai cao 206, 9 hang can 412 -> phai cuon True (vua 9 hang)

F. binh chung ca phong qua mang (bo dong bo that + kenh gia lap)
F1. may KHACH nhan 3 goi 'roi binh' 501 (lap): hien 1 binh; toi gan -> gui 3 goi xin (so hieu 501, ghe 0); chua duoc giao thi chua bay = True, so binh 0
F1b. chu phong giao 501 cho ghe 0 (minh, goi lap 3 lan): binh con = False, so binh mau = 1
F2. binh 502 giao cho ghe 1 (nguoi khac): bien mat = True, lan cuoi cach ho 0.18 m, so binh mana cua minh = 0
F3. goi 'thuoc ve ghe 0' den truoc goi 'roi ra' (binh 503 o 8 m): so binh mau = 2
F4. may CHU PHONG gieo binh 1: gui 3 goi 'roi binh' (lap 3 lan); ghe 1 xin truoc, ghe 2 xin sau -> gui 'thuoc ghe 1' 3 lan, 'thuoc ghe khac' 0 lan; binh dang bay ve ghe 1
F5. chu phong dung gan binh 2: so binh mau 2 -> 3, bao ca phong 'thuoc ghe 0' 3 lan, goi xin tu gui 0
